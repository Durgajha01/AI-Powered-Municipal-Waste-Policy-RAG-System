# E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2023 — Karnataka State Adaptation
Jurisdiction: Karnataka State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB), applicable within BBMP limits
Effective Date: 2023-10-01

## Section 2.1: Definition and Scope
These rules apply to electrical and electronic equipment (EEE) and their components, consumables, parts, and spares that become e-waste, including batteries covered under the Battery Waste Management Rules.

## Section 3.4: Household E-Waste Collection Points
BBMP shall designate at least one authorized e-waste collection point per ward. Residents must deposit e-waste only at authorized collection points or through authorized dismantlers/recyclers, and shall not dispose of e-waste with mixed municipal solid waste.

## Section 3.5: Penalty for Improper E-Waste Disposal
Disposal of e-waste along with regular municipal solid waste, or dumping in public spaces, is prohibited under Section 3.4 and shall attract a fine of INR 1,000 per instance for individuals, and INR 10,000 for bulk consumers such as offices and institutions.

## Section 4.2: Collection Frequency at Ward Collection Points
Authorized e-waste collection points shall be operational at least twice a month, with collected e-waste transported to registered dismantlers within 15 days of receipt.

## Section 5.1: Producer Responsibility
Producers of electrical and electronic equipment are responsible for establishing collection mechanisms and meeting extended producer responsibility (EPR) targets as notified annually by the Central Pollution Control Board.
