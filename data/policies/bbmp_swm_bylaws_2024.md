# BBMP Solid Waste Management Bylaws, 2024
Jurisdiction: Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP)
Effective Date: 2024-04-01

## Section 3.1: Source Segregation Requirement
Every household, commercial establishment, and institution within BBMP limits shall segregate solid waste at source into three categories: wet (biodegradable) waste, dry (recyclable) waste, and domestic hazardous waste. Mixed waste shall not be handed over to collection staff.

## Section 3.2: Segregation Non-Compliance Penalty
A first-time violation of Section 3.1 shall attract a spot fine of INR 100 for individual households and INR 500 for bulk waste generators (establishments generating more than 100 kg of waste per day). Repeat violations within a 90-day period shall attract a fine of INR 500 for households and INR 2,000 for bulk generators, and collection may be suspended for up to 7 days.

## Section 4.2: Ward-Level Awareness Requirement
Where ward-level monitoring data shows that more than 25% of collected waste from a ward is improperly segregated over any rolling 30-day period, the ward's Health Inspector shall initiate a mandatory door-to-door awareness drive within 15 days, in coordination with the local Resident Welfare Association (RWA).

## Section 5.1: Bulk Waste Generator Obligations
Bulk waste generators must process biodegradable waste on-site through composting or biomethanation, or hand it over to BBMP-authorized processing agencies. Non-compliance shall attract a daily penalty of INR 5,000 until compliance is demonstrated.

## Section 6.1: Collection Frequency
Wet waste shall be collected daily (including Sundays) from all notified collection points. Dry waste shall be collected twice a week on a fixed schedule published by the respective ward office.

## Section 7.3: Public Space Littering
Littering or dumping of any waste in public spaces, storm water drains, lakes, or vacant sites is prohibited. Violation shall attract a fine of INR 200 for individuals and INR 2,000 for commercial entities per instance, in addition to mandatory clean-up cost recovery.
