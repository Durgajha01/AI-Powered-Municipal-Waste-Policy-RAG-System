# Single-Use Plastic Ban Order, 2024
Jurisdiction: Karnataka State Government, enforced by BBMP within city limits
Effective Date: 2024-01-01

## Section 1.2: Banned Items
The manufacture, sale, storage, and use of the following single-use plastic items is prohibited: plastic carry bags below 120 microns, plastic cutlery, plastic straws, plastic flags, plastic banners below 100 microns, and thermocol (polystyrene) decorative items.

## Section 2.3: Penalty for Sale or Use of Banned Items
First-time violation by a vendor or commercial establishment shall attract a fine of INR 5,000. Second violation shall attract a fine of INR 10,000. Third and subsequent violations shall attract a fine of INR 25,000 along with potential cancellation of trade license.

## Section 2.4: Individual Use Penalty
An individual found using banned single-use plastic items in public shall be fined INR 200 per instance.

## Section 3.1: Enforcement Mechanism
BBMP Marshals and designated Health Inspectors are authorized to conduct surprise inspections at commercial establishments, wholesale markets, and public events to check compliance.
