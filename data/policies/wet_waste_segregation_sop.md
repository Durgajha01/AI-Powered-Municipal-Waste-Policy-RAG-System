# Wet Waste Segregation Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
Jurisdiction: BBMP, issued by Solid Waste Management Cell
Effective Date: 2024-04-01

## Section A.1: Household Composting Encouragement
Households generating less than 5 kg of wet waste per day are encouraged to adopt home composting using pit, pot, or vermicompost methods. BBMP provides subsidized composting kits through ward offices.

## Section A.2: Apartment Complex Requirements
Apartment complexes and gated communities with more than 50 units must install and operate an on-site organic waste converter (OWC) or community composting unit with a minimum processing capacity matching 80% of the complex's estimated daily wet waste generation.

## Section B.1: Monitoring and Reporting
Ward Health Inspectors shall conduct random waste audits at collection points at least twice a month, recording the proportion of segregated versus mixed waste received. Data shall be uploaded to the BBMP Solid Waste Dashboard within 48 hours of the audit.

## Section B.2: Escalation Threshold
If a ward's audit data shows a segregation compliance rate below 75% for two consecutive audit cycles, the ward shall be flagged for escalated enforcement under Bylaw Section 4.2, including intensified awareness drives and increased spot-fine enforcement.
